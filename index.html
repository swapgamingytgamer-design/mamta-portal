Principal's Message

This year marks a monumental milestone for Mamta Modern Senior Secondary School as we proudly celebrate the Golden Jubilee of our inception. Adding to this momentous occasion, I am deeply honored to have been awarded the prestigious National Teachers Award.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to the Almighty, our visionary management, supportive parents, and dedicated students for their unwavering support. This accolade is a testament to the collective dedication of our school community.

Over the years, our school has consistently excelled—from outstanding CBSE Board Results to victories in Zonal and Inter-Zonal Competitions. As we celebrate, we reaffirm our commitment to fostering innovation and sustainability, aligning our vision with the UN’s Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

To my dear students, I urge you to continue striving for excellence. Together, we will shape a future filled with promise and achievement.

Best wishes for an extraordinary year ahead!
