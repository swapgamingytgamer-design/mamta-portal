Principal's Message

“Excellence is not a destination; it is a continuous journey that never ends.” – Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

This year marks a monumental milestone for Mamta Modern Senior Secondary School as we proudly celebrate the Golden Jubilee of our inception. Adding to this momentous occasion, I am deeply honored to have been awarded the prestigious National Teachers Award.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to the Almighty, our visionary management, supportive parents, and dedicated students for their unwavering support in making this achievement possible. This accolade is not just a personal honor but a testament to the collective dedication and efforts of our school community.

Over the years, our school has consistently excelled in various fields, with remarkable accomplishments in academics and co-curricular domains. From outstanding CBSE Board Results and Science accolades to sports achievements and victories in Zonal and Inter-Zonal Competitions, our students have demonstrated exceptional dedication and potential.

As we celebrate this remarkable recognition, we are mindful of the responsibilities it brings. We reaffirm our commitment to fostering a culture of innovation, inclusivity, and sustainability, aligning our vision with the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

To my dear students, I urge you to continue striving for excellence. Together, we will shape a future filled with promise and achievement.
