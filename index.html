Welcome to Mamta Modern Sr. Sec. School

Established in 1973, Mamta Modern Senior Secondary School has journeyed from its humble beginnings to become one of the most prestigious institutions in West Delhi. With a steadfast commitment to excellence, the school has continuously evolved, boasting state-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities essential for modern education.

Recognized by the Directorate of Education, Government of NCT, Delhi, the school achieved affiliation with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) in 1986 (Affiliation No. 2730133). Today, it offers comprehensive education in three streams: Science, Commerce, and Humanities, catering to the diverse academic interests of its students.

The school was founded by the Mamta Modern Education Society, a legally registered body operating on a permanent basis under the relevant provisions of law. This foundation has been instrumental in establishing the institution’s ethos of holistic education and sustained growth over the decades.
