Our Legacy

Established in 1973, Mamta Modern School has emerged as a prestigious institution in West Delhi. Recognized by the Directorate of Education, Govt of NCT, Delhi, and affiliated with the CBSE (Affiliation No. 2730133) since 1986, we impart quality education across Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams.

The school is managed by the Mamta Modern Education Society and guided by a Managing Committee of experts. Shri Manoj Sharma serves as the present Manager of the school.

Campus & Infrastructure

Our campus spans approximately 4 acres of DDA-allotted land, featuring a towering building and lush green lawns. We provide an environment that is both visually soothing and intellectually stimulating for our students.

Sports Facilities

7300 Sq. Meter playground. Proud winners of the "SUBROTO CUP" (1998). Nursery for players like Sunil Chhetri, Indian Football Team Captain.

Learning Resources

A massive library with over 18,000 books covering History, Sports, Culture, and more to keep students and staff updated.

Transport

A fleet of 10 self-owned and 5 hired buses, accompanied by responsible staff members to ensure student safety.

Special Facilities

Gymnasium, Music Rooms, Dance Rooms, and comprehensive Health & Medical checkup facilities.
