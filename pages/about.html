About Our School

The school was established in the year 1973 and it never saw behind during its journey to the present prestigious institution of West Delhi having up to date ultra modern and sufficient infrastructure needed for running a successful school. It was recognized by the Directorate of Education, Govt of NCT, Delhi and now stands affiliated with the CBSE vide affiliation no. 2730133 since 1986. The school imparts education in 3 streams i.e. Science, Commerce and Humanities.

Management & Leadership

The school was established by the Mamta Modern Education Society duly registered under the relevant provisions of law on permanent basis. The school is managed by the Managing Committee consisting of members having special knowledge in the different fields of the life. Shri Manoj Sharma is the present manager of the school.

Infrastructure & Facilities

Very spacious and towering building giving an attractive look stand on the plot. Lush green lawns dot the school premises giving soothing effect to the eyes of the students. A very spacious Play ground measuring approximately 7300 Square Meter is available. Besides, facilities for Indoor Games, Dance Rooms, Gymnasium, Music Rooms, Health and Medical checkup facilities are provided.

Sports Legacy

Special attention is given to promote Sports which has resulted into winning Laurels. It can boast of lifting “SUBROTO CUP” in the year 1998 and has given birth to international players like Sunil Chhetri, Captain of the Indian Football Team. The school has been adopted by the Sports Authority of India (SAI).
