Our Heritage Since 1973

Established in 1973, Mamta Modern Senior Secondary School has evolved into a prestigious institution in West Delhi. Affiliated with the CBSE (Affiliation No. 2730133) since 1986, we impart education across Science, Commerce, and Humanities.

Visionary Leadership

Managed by the Mamta Modern Education Society, the school thrives under the guidance of educationists. Shri Manoj Sharma serves as the Manager, ensuring the school maintains its ultra-modern infrastructure.

The Sports Nursery

Our school is a nursery for international talent. We made history by lifting the “SUBROTO CUP” in 1998. We are proud to be the alma mater of Indian National Football Captain, Sunil Chhetri. Our facilities are adopted by the Sports Authority of India (SAI).
