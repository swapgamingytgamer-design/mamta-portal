School History & Affiliation

The school was established in the year 1973 and it never saw behind during its journey to the present prestigious institution of West Delhi having up to date ultra modern and sufficient infrastructure needed for running a successful school.

It was recognized by the Directorate of Education, Govt of NCT, Delhi and now stands affiliated with the CBSE vide affiliation no. 2730133 since 1986. The school imparts education in 3 streams i.e. Science, Commerce and Humanities.

Infrastructure & Campus

The school stands on a plot of land measuring approximately 4 acres allotted by the DDA. Very spacious and towering building giving an attractive look stand on the plot. Lush green lawns dot the school premises giving soothing effect to the eyes of the students.

A very spacious Playground measuring approximately 7300 Square Meter is available. We can boast of lifting the “SUBROTO CUP” in the year 1998 and have given birth to International level players like Sunil Chhetri, the Captain of the Indian Football Team.

Management & Safety

Shri Manoj Sharma is the present manager of the school. The school maintains a library with more than 18,000 books and specialized cells for student safety.
