About Our Institution

Our Journey

Established in 1973, Mamta Modern School has grown into a prestigious institution in West Delhi. Recognized by the Directorate of Education and affiliated with CBSE (No. 2730133) since 1986, we offer excellence in Science, Commerce, and Humanities.

Managed by the Mamta Modern Education Society, the school sits on a lush 4-acre plot allotted by the DDA, featuring towering buildings and serene greenery.

Sports & Excellence

Our 7300 Sq. Meter playground is a nursery for international talent. We are proud to have nurtured Indian Football Legend Sunil Chhetri. From lifting the "SUBROTO CUP" in 1998 to winning national laurels every year, our dedicated coaches for Football, Basketball, and more ensure our students lead the nation.

Infrastructure & Safety

We boast a library of over 18,000 books, ultra-modern labs, and 15 dedicated buses (10 self-owned) to ensure safe and adequate transport for all students.
