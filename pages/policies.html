2. Fee & Online Payment Policy

The school accepts online fees via the ICICI Payment Gateway. Fees once paid are non-refundable. The school is not responsible for transaction failures due to technical errors on the user's end or bank server issues.

Fee structure is reviewed annually by the Managing Committee in consultation with financial experts.

3. Anti-Bullying & Sexual Harassment Policy

Mamta Modern School maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy towards bullying and harassment. An Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) headed by the Principal is active to address any grievances. We strictly follow the guidelines issued by the Appropriate Authority to ensure a safe environment for every student.

4. Transport Safety Guidelines

Our fleet of 15 buses (10 self-owned, 5 hired) is equipped with GPS and monitored by responsible staff members. Students must adhere to the discipline code inside the buses. Changes in transport routes must be requested in writing at least 15 days in advance.
