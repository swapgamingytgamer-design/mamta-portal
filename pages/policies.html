School Policies & Terms

Privacy Policy

Mamta Modern Sr. Sec. School respects your privacy and ensures the protection of your personal information. We collect basic details such as student name, parent name, contact number, and payment details for fee processing.

  • All online payments are processed through a secure ICICI Bank payment gateway, ensuring encrypted transactions.
  • We do not store any sensitive payment details such as card numbers, CVV, or net banking credentials.
  • Personal information will not be shared with third parties except as required for payment processing or legal compliance.
  • The website uses appropriate security measures to safeguard user data.

Online Fee Payment Terms

By using the online fee payment service, you agree to the following:

  • Fees once paid through the ICICI payment gateway shall be considered final, subject to school policies.
  • The school is not responsible for transaction failures due to network issues, bank server problems, or technical errors.
  • Users must ensure correct entry of student details before making payment.
  • Any applicable transaction charges imposed by the bank/payment gateway shall be borne by the user.
  • The school reserves the right to update terms without prior notice.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • Fee payments once made are non-refundable, except in exceptional cases as per school management approval.
  • In case of duplicate payment or failed transaction with deducted amount, the issue must be reported to the school office with proof of transaction.
  • Approved refunds, if any, will be processed through the original mode of payment within a reasonable time.
  • No cancellation is allowed once the payment is successfully processed.
