We Build the Nation

From the roots of seventy-three,
A journey of excellence, for all to see.
In the green lawns where dreams take flight,
We learn to lead with all our might.
With books in hand and a goal in view,
We build the nation, bold and new.

— By A Senior Student

On the Field of Gold

Seven thousand meters of green and wide,
Where champions play with heart and pride.
From Subroto's win to Chhetri's name,
We play for love, we play for fame.
Under the gold and midnight blue,
Every Mamta student stays true.

— By Junior Literary Club
